# Summary

This data analysis project explores the Olist E-commerce dataset using SQL queries executed in MySQL Workbench. The primary goal was to derive actionable business insights from structured relational data, helping inform strategic decisions in sales, operations, customer experience, and marketing.

---

## Key Findings & Insights

### 1. **Top Revenue Contributors**
Product categories like **health_beauty**, **watches_gifts**, and **bed_bath_table** generated the highest total revenue. These segments are strong revenue drivers and ideal candidates for promotions, bundling, or inventory prioritization.

### 2. **Customer Retention & Loyalty**
Multiple customers placed **more than one order**, indicating **repeat purchase behavior**. These loyal customers represent an opportunity for targeted loyalty campaigns and personalized retention strategies.

### 3. **Yearly Growth in Orders**
Order volume grew significantly from **2016 to 2018**, reflecting strong business expansion and customer acquisition over time. Strategic planning can be aligned with this positive growth trajectory.

### 4. **Customer Satisfaction Patterns**
Categories such as **musical_instruments**, **stationery**, and **audio** received the highest **average review scores**, reflecting higher satisfaction and quality perceptions. These insights support product expansion or recommendation systems.

### 5. **Efficient Deliveries by Category**
Items like **office_furniture**, **garden_tools**, and **fashion_shoes** showed the **fastest average delivery times**. These products benefit from reliable logistics and may be used to benchmark slower categories.

### 6. **Geographic Sales Concentration**
The **states of São Paulo (SP)** and **Rio de Janeiro (RJ)** dominated total sales volume, confirming them as major consumer hubs. These regions can be focal points for logistics investments and local marketing.

### 7. **Preferred Payment Methods**
**Credit cards** were the most used and highest revenue-generating payment method, followed by **boleto** and **voucher**. These insights aid in refining checkout experiences and payment gateway partnerships.

---

## Business Implications

- **Customer loyalty programs** can be designed around repeat buyers.
- **Marketing efforts** should focus on high-performing product categories and geographies.
- **Operational improvements** can be made by studying categories with faster delivery.
- **Product and UX decisions** should consider top-rated categories and preferred payment types.

---

This SQL-based analysis confirms the **power of structured data analytics** in uncovering patterns that support strategic decision-making in E-commerce. By leveraging clean SQL queries and joining across relational tables, deep insights were revealed efficiently and accurately.
